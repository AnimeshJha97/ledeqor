# Module 14: AI Engineer Interview Preparation

## Module Purpose

This module turns your learning and capstone into interview performance.

You are positioning as a senior full-stack engineer who can build production-grade AI applications, not as a beginner who only tried prompts.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this module, you should be able to:

- explain your MERN-to-AI transition
- introduce Arkion DocIntel clearly
- answer Python, FastAPI, LLM, RAG, vector DB, agent, eval, security, and system design questions
- tell strong project stories
- handle leadership and salary conversations
- run mock interviews with a checklist

## Final Mini Build

Create:

- final interview pitch
- project deep-dive script
- system design answer
- 50-question practice bank
- STAR stories
- mock interview checklist

## 14.1 How AI Engineer Interviews Are Structured

Typical rounds:

- recruiter screen
- technical screen
- coding or backend round
- AI system design
- project deep dive
- behavioral round
- hiring manager round

## 14.2 How To Explain Your Transition From MERN To AI

Pitch:

"I have five years of full-stack product experience. I expanded into applied AI by building a document intelligence platform with Python, FastAPI, LLMs, embeddings, vector search, RAG, workflows, evaluation, and deployment."

## 14.3 How To Introduce Yourself For AI Roles

Keep it tight:

- years of experience
- core full-stack strengths
- applied AI focus
- capstone proof
- target role

4. AI pipeline.
5. Reliability and security.
