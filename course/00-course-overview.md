# Course Overview

## Working Product

Orvion DocIntel: AI Document Intelligence Platform for Businesses.

## Who This Course Is For

This course is for a senior MERN full-stack engineer with around 5 years of experience who wants to transition into applied AI engineering without pretending to be a pure ML researcher.

The target identity is:

Senior Full Stack Engineer + Applied AI Engineer

## What You Will Build

You will build a production-style B2B SaaS platform where companies can upload business documents and use AI to:

- Understand documents
- Extract important fields
- Classify document types
- Search across documents
- Ask questions with citations
- Compare multiple documents
- Generate summaries
- Detect risks and missing information
- Create workflows from documents
- Export structured data
