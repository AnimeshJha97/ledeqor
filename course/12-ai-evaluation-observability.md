# Module 10: AI Evaluation, Testing & Observability

## Purpose

Learn how professionals measure extraction accuracy, retrieval quality, hallucination risk, citation correctness, latency, token usage, and cost.

## Evaluation Examples

- Invoice total amount extracted correctly or not
- Contract effective date extracted correctly or not
- RAG answer supported by document source or not
- Citation points to correct page or not
- System refuses answer when document lacks information or not
- Summary misses critical risk or not
